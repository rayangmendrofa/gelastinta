Sheet 05 — Talk & reviews

Talks

logic ⇄ laughter

“Talk” is the space where I sit down with ideas, whether they come from research papers, music compositions, or everyday curiosities and give them a voice. It’s part review, part reflection, and part playful debate with myself. Here, I attempt to break down complex topics into accessible stories, sprinkle in scientific humor, and add my own commentary to show how engineering, science, and creativity connect in unexpected ways. Think of it as a conversation between logic and laughter, where serious insights meet lighthearted observations, and every topic becomes a chance to explore the rhythm of knowledge.
