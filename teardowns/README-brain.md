# gt-brain-figure (Talk 12, Learning brain)

`gt-brain-figure/` builds the interactive figure served as `src/static/teardowns/learning-brain.html`: an imaginary bench diffusion rig around one 2 mm voxel of brain, with a four key pad for the finger task, a carriage that sets the scan time (before, 30 min, 24 h after learning), a region knob, and four dial gauges (MD, then the SANDI soma, neurite and extracellular fractions). It is an anatomy kit Figure (isometric SVG, no WebGL). `model.mjs` holds every number and says where it comes from: group means read off Fig 3 of Griffa et al., PLOS Biology 24(6): e3003861 (2026), CC BY 4.0, plus the scan and task settings from the Methods. Dial scales and the ten times magnified cartoon on the cube are stated assumptions. Build with `ANATOMY=<anatomy kit folder> node teardowns/gt-brain-figure/build.mjs` from the repo root; `?pose=region,time` (for example `?pose=3,2`) freezes a pose for stills. The poster `src/assets/new-brain-poster.png` is `?pose=3,2` (PPC, 24 h) at 1067 × 600, scale 1.5, cropped to the card.
