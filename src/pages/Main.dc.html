02 — About

About

This is a visual interpretation of my works and hobbies, some things are essential in my professional persona and some are just fun, quirky drop of thoughts.

While it’s important to highlight skills and journey of my life, I find comfort in sharing bits of my interest, from short thoughts to full-length rants.

Much of how I think was shaped by Richard Feynman, and one line of his stays with me more than any equation:

“The highest forms of understanding we can achieve are laughter and human compassion.”

— Richard P. Feynman

It reminds me that science doesn’t have to be cold: the best ideas make people smile and bring them closer. And over the years, the most valuable thing I have learned is not to answer every question, but to ask better questions.
