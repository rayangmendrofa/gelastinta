01 · The plant

What it controls

Water

Drip irrigation

Four zones. A cycle starts when enough sunlight has added up, or when the maximum interval runs out.

Nutrients

Fertigation

Inline A and B nutrient and acid dosing on EC and pH. The dose follows the flow, with a PI trim on top. Two pH probes check each other.

Air

Climate

Roof vents, a shade net, fogging and pad and fan cooling. A state machine runs them, with overrides for rain, wind and storms.

Safety

Protection

E-stop monitoring, pump dry run and pressure limits, crop protection limits on EC and pH, lockouts when a motor gives no feedback, and NAMUR NE 43 signal fault detection.

02 · The setup

How it was built

greenhouse.stIEC 61131-3 Structured Text→ OpenPLC v4runs the program⇄ Modbus TCPthe I/O link⇄ Virtual plantstands in for the hardware

The program is IEC 61131-3 Structured Text for OpenPLC v4, connected over Modbus TCP. A virtual plant replaces the hardware. It models the weather, the greenhouse air, the crop, the hydraulics and the water chemistry. An ISA-101 style web HMI shows it running.

05 · Play

Play it in your browser

Play in your browser ▸

The same greenhouse.st runs in your browser, scan by scan, against a model of the greenhouse. You grow crops, expand to three greenhouses that each run their own copy of the program with no change to the code, and buy upgrades that switch on PLC functions that were parked until the hardware was there. Things break, some where you can see them and some only in the numbers, and you diagnose and repair them.

Open full screen ↗

Works on phones too. Your progress is saved in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. The game ends with its own “What is real and what is game” section.

06 · Honest limits

Limitations

  • It has not yet run against physical wiring.
  • The E-stop handling is a monitoring layer only. Real equipment needs a hardwired, certified safety circuit.
  • Modbus TCP has no authentication, so keep it on an isolated network.
