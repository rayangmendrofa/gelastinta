Sheet 02 — The workshop

Projects

Projects is the workshop of my website, this space is where ideas take shape and become tangible. Here I share the things I’ve built, designed, and explored: from engineering prototypes and CAD models to infographics, research papers, and creative experiments. Each project reflects my curiosity and drive to blend technical precision with playful imagination.

This section isn’t just a portfolio; it’s a narrative of process and discovery. You’ll find detailed designs alongside visual stories, scientific reviews paired with creative commentary, and experiments that connect engineering with broader human experiences. Whether it’s a mechanical concept, a data visualization, or a paper that sparked new insights, every project is part of my journey to make complex ideas accessible, engaging, and meaningful.
