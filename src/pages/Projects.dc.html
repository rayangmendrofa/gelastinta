Sheet 02 · The workshop

Projects

Projects is the workshop of my website. This is where ideas take shape and become something you can look at. I share the things I’ve built, designed and explored, from engineering prototypes and CAD models to infographics, research papers and creative experiments. Each one comes from my curiosity and my wish to mix technical precision with a bit of play.

This section is a record of how I work and what I find out along the way. You’ll find detailed designs next to visual stories, and scientific reviews next to my own commentary. Some experiments connect engineering with wider human experiences. Whether it’s a mechanical concept, a data visualisation or a paper that gave me a new idea, I try to make it easy to follow and worth your time.
