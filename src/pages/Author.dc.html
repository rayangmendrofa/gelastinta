Sheet 04 — About the author

Rayang Termedate Mendrofa

My name is Rayang Termedate Mendrofa, a mechanical engineer with a passion for turning ideas into reality. While the main concept of engineering is closely related to systems, machines, and building technology. my work isn’t just about technical rigor — it’s about creativity. Whether I’m designing a robotic contraption, a sequence of experiments , or composing playful narratives and music, I thrive at the intersection of engineering precision and imaginative storytelling.

I believe science and music share a rhythm: both are languages of discovery, emotion, and connection. This site is my canvas to share projects, reviews, papers, and presentations — each reflecting my curiosity and drive to make technology more accessible, sustainable, and inspiring. It’s not just a portfolio; it’s a space where ideas meet humour, and where engineering becomes a story worth telling.
