Industry project, 2026

A batch tank with no cook

2026Personal projectPLCIEC 61131-3 Structured TextOpenPLC · Modbus TCPKiCad 9

A heated 40 litre mixing tank that fills, stirs, heats, holds and drains on its own. The PLC program runs against a virtual plant that misbehaves on purpose, and 66 automated tests check that it stays safe when wires break and sensors lie. The I/O board that would replace the virtual plant is drawn in KiCad.

02 · The big idea

Let the sequence ask, let the interlocks decide

Chapter 4 of the report explains why

An output needs two things: a request from the sequence and an interlock word of zero. Break a condition and watch the heater.

Figure A · Try to switch the heater on

E-101 heater: allowed

ilk_heat = 2#000000 (16#0000)

Nothing is missing, so the request goes through and the PI controller drives the heater.

The six heater conditions and their bit order follow the program and the HMI reason texts.

03 · The setup

Three programs and one board

Chapters 3 and 11 of the report

The PLC never knows if the plant is a program or real wires. Swap the box on the right and nothing else changes.

Figure B · Who talks to whom

Today: a virtual plant

The plant is a Python program with slow valves, contactor delays and faults to inject. It answers Modbus the way a real remote I/O coupler would.

05 · The evidence

Proving it without a PLC

Chapters 8 and 9 of the report

The real program file runs in a test bench against a virtual plant that breaks on purpose. Then the same file ran on the real OpenPLC runtime.
