03 — Questions

FAQ

Click a question to open it

How does a project work?
  1. Kickoff. I read your paper and we have a 30 minute call about the message and the audience.
  2. Outline and sketches. You approve the outline and rough figure sketches before any design starts.
  3. Draft. I write the text and design the figures.
  4. Two revision rounds, included in every package.
  5. Accuracy check. You, or the researcher, sign off that the science is right.
  6. Delivery. Final files: PNG, PDF and SVG, plus HTML for interactive work.
What do I need to send you?
  • The paper, thesis or data
  • The one message people should remember
  • Who the audience is
  • Your logo and colours, if you have them
  • One contact person who approves things
What isn’t included?
  • New research or data analysis beyond your source
  • Printing, or hosting on your site
  • Rewrites after the outline is approved (these count as an extra revision)
How do payments work?

A 50% deposit to start, and the rest on delivery. Prices are in US dollars; I can also quote in Rupiah.

How do the bundle discounts work?

Every service and extra counts as an item. 2 items get 10% off, 3 items 15%, and 4 or more 20%. Use Build your quote to see your price.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds are included. More rounds are $6 (Rp 75rb) each.

How long does it take?

From 3 to 5 days for a visual abstract or poster, up to 2 to 4 weeks for an interactive explainer. Rush delivery is available for 25% extra.

Who owns the final work?

You do. I may show it in my portfolio, unless you ask me to keep it private.

Can you use figures from published papers?

I redraw figures from scratch, or use your own. Many published figures have licences that don’t allow changes or commercial use, so I don’t copy them.

Can you work in Bahasa Indonesia?

Yes. A second language version (Bahasa Indonesia and English) adds 30%.
