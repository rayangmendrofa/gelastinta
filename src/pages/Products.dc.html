02 — The job, step by step

How it works

The same for every package

  1. 1
    KickoffI read your paper and we have a 30 minute call about the message and the audience.
  2. 2
    Outline and sketchesYou approve the outline and rough figure sketches before any design starts.
  3. 3
    DraftI write the text and design the figures.
  4. 4
    Two revision roundsIncluded in every package.
  5. 5
    Accuracy checkYou, or the researcher, sign off that the science is right.
  6. 6
    DeliveryFinal files: PNG, PDF and SVG, plus HTML for interactive work.

You provide

  • The paper, thesis or data
  • The one message people should remember
  • Who the audience is
  • Your logo and colours, if any
  • One contact person who approves things

Not included

  • New research or data analysis beyond the source
  • Printing, or hosting on your site
  • Rewrites after the outline is approved (charged as a revision)
