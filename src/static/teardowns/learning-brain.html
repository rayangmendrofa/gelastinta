My own drawing of an imaginary bench instrument, built on the paper’s numbers. The real scan is a whole head in the 3T Connectome scanner (300 mT/m gradients, 2 mm voxels, 7 b values from 200 to 8,000 s/mm² plus b = 0 images). Dials, left to right at the back: MD on its own post (one full turn = 10 % change), then soma, neurite and extracellular fractions (one full turn = 0.04 of the signal). Values are group means read off Fig 3 of Griffa et al., PLOS Biology 24(6): e3003861 (2026), CC BY 4.0, so the third decimal is approximate. The cells drawn on the cube are a cartoon, with their changes magnified ten times. The real training took about 15 to 20 minutes; here a block lasts under half a second.
